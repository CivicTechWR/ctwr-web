## Project updates

**Connected KW** made a GitHub repository with its projects and tasks and started getting everyone set up. The goal is to finish the profile form and the request form. Next the team will hand out tasks and make sure new volunteers can get the project running on their own computers.

**Midtown Radio** made a Kanban board and added structure to its problem statement with flow charts and documentation. The team chose Flutter to build the app. Next it's waiting on mockups from Midtown Radio, handing out tasks, and writing a script to help set up the development environment.

**Our research and booth team** started planning for the Youth Tech Labs Demo Day on April 12 ([discussion here](https://discourse.ctwr.org/t/youth-tech-labs-demo-day-saturday-april-12th-ctwr-booth/72)). They also built a simple word cloud that could grow into an interactive kiosk for the booth, and they're open to help with it.
