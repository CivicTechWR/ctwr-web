This week Jessica, who leads project support at CivicTechWR, gave a talk on how she runs projects with volunteer teams. Her advice included:

- Decide early what your project is for and how you'll know it's working.
- Keep plans and conversations where everyone can see them, for example in GitHub Projects and Discussions.
- Prioritize tasks by how urgent they are, not just by their deadline.
- When you hand out tasks, pay attention to what makes each person feel their time is well spent.

## Project updates

**LoMo** set up project management, listed the features in progress, and worked on the messaging screen and other small bugs. Next up is working through feedback on the design and on how people move through the app.

**Home Hub** made progress on password reset and on adding terms of service to sign-up, and started work on handling incoming email. The team is also working on the parts of a pilot launch that aren't code: a landing page, a video, and supporting content.

**Waterloo Region Votes** has a new poster. Volunteers are adding timestamps to recordings of all-candidates meetings, and research and data entry on candidate questionnaires continues. The team could use help putting posters up around the region.

**One Million Neighbours** uploaded a test file to ConnectedKW to help pull in real data, and is getting everyone set up to run the project on their own computers. Next is putting that data on a map and talking through the user experience.

**Accommodation** published its questionnaire and made a QR code for it. Next the team plans to build a landing page and share the results publicly as statistical reports.

**Beside** kept narrowing down what to build. The team considered celebrating shared progress when friends reach a goal together, and decided against money-based rewards.

**SafeQR** won't continue this season.

## Get involved

If you live in Ward 5, ask your neighbourhood association to record its all-candidates meeting. It helps Waterloo Region Votes share the meeting with voters who couldn't attend.

On December 2, the University of Waterloo's Engineering and Peace class will help our teams with user testing.

A CBC reporter is also open to hearing about other local civic tech projects.
