Week 2 was about planning. Teams used the evening to decide what they can realistically finish this season and who's going to do it.

## Project updates

**Waterloo Region Votes** emailed candidates the night before, and 11% had already replied by the time we met. With the election coming up fast, the team needs help in three places:

- following candidates on Facebook and Instagram to keep the events page current
- tracking down candidate questionnaires that local organizations publish
- recording all-candidates meetings on a phone. Four of them are on September 27, and we can lend you a tripod.

**One Million Neighbours** settled its scope for the season. It'll start with letting people choose their own neighbourhood boundaries and add more if time allows. The team has a good mix of skills and is tracking work on GitHub.

**LoMo** set a clear goal for the season: a usable app with a readable design, stronger admin controls, and access limited to the community it serves. The team is turning its to-do list into GitHub issues and would love help with testing. That means using the app and writing down anything that breaks or confuses you.

**Home Hub** made progress on two milestone tasks, including a feature that lets housing providers say a home is open to accessibility changes.

**Accommodation** finished its questionnaire and is getting ready to share it with a QR code and posters around town.

**SafeQR** found real cases of QR code scams in Stratford and Kelowna. The next step is finding a local organization interested in trying it.

**Beside** spent the evening defining the idea. It may come back as a project in a future season once its scope is clearer.
