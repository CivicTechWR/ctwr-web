Season 4 opened with guests. Students from the University of Waterloo who study sustainable business and social innovation presented system maps of challenges and opportunities in our local economy. The maps look at how social enterprises, community wealth-building, and local economic development affect each other. Small groups then talked through each map.

## Homelessness in the region

One student presented a system map of homelessness in Waterloo Region. A guest from the Plan to End Chronic Homelessness (PECH) pointed out causes the map could add, such as renoviction, and the room had plenty of constructive feedback. The two plan to keep talking.

## Startups

People from very different backgrounds compared notes on startups. Topics included which markets Canadian startups could look to and how non-tech startups can take advantage of supply chains. The group's takeaway was that plenty of help exists for founders, and a clear roadmap and playbook go a long way toward reducing uncertainty.

## Climate change in Southern Ontario

Organizing gets harder as the region grows toward a million people. The group talked about building up instead of out and about working with government on new policy. Next they want to dig into urbanization.
