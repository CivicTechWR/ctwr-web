## Project updates

**One Million Neighbours** worked on loading data into its database, worked with the geographic data, and started analyzing it. The map is coming together nicely. Next the team will document its process so the work goes faster.

**PECH** has a first visual design, and its repository is live with everyone set up. The team worked on the back end and on webhooks. Next is more work on the front end, a closer review of it, and back-end work for processing data.

**Project Ploughshares** finished a first pass at the interface, got everyone set up in the repository, and made more engineering decisions. Next is collecting and loading data, testing the interface, and fixing what turns up.
