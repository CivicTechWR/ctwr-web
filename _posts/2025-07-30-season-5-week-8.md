## Project updates

**PECH** has a landing page. Next the team will improve it and add the rest of the site's pages.

**Project Ploughshares** worked on an AI crawler that collects weapons export data from MarketLine. The team ran into trouble processing PDFs, so it plans to build a crawler just for PDFs and test it on samples where the right answer is already known. Next it will make a shared repository for the code, welcome more volunteers, and look for more data sources using advanced Google searches.

**One Million Neighbours** welcomed new volunteers, and two of them turned Statistics Canada data into something the project can use. The team also talked about how to keep the project going after launch. Next is getting the new data onto the map.

**Our organizers** drafted the agenda for the September showcase and settled on its purpose. They also drafted a sponsorship package and worked on a "Built with CivicTech Waterloo Region" tag that projects can put on their websites. Next are gold, silver, and bronze badges for sponsors, and getting the event out on social media and digital posters.
