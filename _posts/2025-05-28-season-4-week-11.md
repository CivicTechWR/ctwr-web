Demo Day is next week! This was our biggest night of the season, with 24 people in the room.

## Project updates

**Midtown Radio**'s Apple developer account is verified, and the app store listing now has screenshots and a description. The team also moved the show feed out of a CivicTechWR GitHub repository to a place the Midtown Radio team can manage on their own. Next is a presentation for Demo Day, and they want plenty of honest feedback on it.

**Connected KW** did a lot of testing and caught some bugs, but overall everything works. The team rewrote the site's text in plain language for people who aren't technical. The skill selection feature still needs work and will be ready for the demo.

**Our organizers** held a rehearsal, and the sound setup works (we think!). Card payments work too, so we can accept donations now. We confirmed a check-in desk downstairs with the building and set up virtual polling for next week. All that's left is the logistics.

## Coming up

Both teams will demo their work at Demo Day on June 4.
