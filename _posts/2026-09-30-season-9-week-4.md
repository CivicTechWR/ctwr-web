On the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, we welcomed Elder Wanda Wilson of the Mohawk Nation, Turtle Clan.

Wanda spoke about what the day means and about the lasting harm residential schools did to individuals, families, and communities. She talked about facing uncomfortable truths, what it means to forgive, and why preserving language and culture matters. She also shared her own path as a mother, a nurse, and a guest educator. A short summary can't do it justice, and we're grateful she spent the evening with us.

To learn more about the Turtle building Wanda mentioned, visit [niagaraturtle.org](https://niagaraturtle.org/).
