This week each team scored its own project out of 20, with up to 5 points in each of four areas: how involved the community partner is, the strength of the team, how interesting the tech is, and how much it matters to the community.

## The scores

**PECH, 19.5 out of 20.** The community partners have committed to showing up, and the team has a good mix of developers and other skills. The data problem is an interesting one, and homelessness affects the whole community. The team tagged its task board and will start looking at the data next.

**Project Ploughshares, 18 out of 20.** Our contact at Ploughshares is available as needed, and the team has lots of energy. The tech has room to grow over the long term, and the cause is a good one: making the arms trade and where weapons end up visible to the public. The team drafted a rough timeline and will track its work against it.

**One Million Neighbours, 17 out of 20.** The project's community partner has joined every other week. The team has three or four people, though who shows up varies. There was a good discussion about building on Connected KW's existing work. A second phase would follow up with 100 people before reaching out to the wider community. The team tagged its task board and will start looking at the data next.
