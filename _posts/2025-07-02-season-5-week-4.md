## Project updates

**Project Ploughshares** had nine people this week and split into groups for the database, the interface, and collecting data. There's a first draft of the database schema and the interface, and the team settled on its tech stack. Next it will keep working in those streams ask Ploughshares for more information, and send them an update.

**PECH** had eight people. The team created its GitHub repository, chose its tech stack, reviewed the data and its options for using it, and designed the workflow for bringing data in. Work on that workflow continues.

**One Million Neighbours** had five people. The team downloaded Statistics Canada data and looked into cleaning it up. Next it will load the data into Supabase.

## Get involved

One Million Neighbours got stuck on map boundary files (shapefiles) and asked for help from anyone who has worked with them.
