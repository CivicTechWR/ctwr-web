## Project updates

**One Million Neighbours** welcomed two volunteers who are working on the data. The team built a view of parks and population for each postal area, which shows the areas that have no parks at all. A new member is building an interactive map. Next the team wants to show the data in a clean table, make the interface nicer, and connect it to the database.

**PECH** worked on the story it wants its data to tell and on its project plan. The team also posted a quick survey in our Slack to see which logo people like best. Next it will set up a repository for the site's content and write it, plan the work so it's done in five weeks, work out how to calculate the metrics from the data, and deploy the site.

**Project Ploughshares** worked through how to collect data from its sources automatically. The team compared AI model APIs and their rate limits, and will probably use Gemini. They also talked about how to get reliable, structured results from the model's output. Next they'll set up authentication and send what the scraper collects to the database they already have.
