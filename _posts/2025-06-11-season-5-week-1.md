Season 5 is underway. Teams spent the first night figuring out what they're building.

## Project updates

**Project Ploughshares** wrote out its problem statement and split the work into streams, with two volunteers sharing the lead. Next is checking in with the team's contact at Ploughshares.

**PECH** wants to build a website that informs people about homelessness in the region. It will share lived experiences, involve several organizations, and be open about the data while keeping people anonymous. The team reviewed a first dataset and is working on getting access to more.

**One Million Neighbours** put a structure in place for the project and is still defining its scope.

**A malware detection tool** that uses AI is in the brainstorming stage.

**Our organizers** went over how Demo Day went and set a date for the end of the season. Next they'll take stock of our equipment and start tracking expenses.
