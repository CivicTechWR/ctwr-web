## Project updates

**Midtown Radio**'s on-demand listening tab works. The team is updating the design to match Midtown Radio's style guide, and it welcomed new volunteers and gave them tasks. Still to do: skipping forward and back, swiping the bottom bar to move to the next episode, updating shows automatically from RSS feeds, and fixing a bug where the app forgets your light or dark mode choice.

**Connected KW** brainstormed marketing and outreach. Ideas included focusing on students, making better use of Instagram and other social media, and connecting with companies that are hiring. On the build side, the team is working on the profile and request forms, organizing the tagging system, creating the flows for email notifications, and reviewing the database structure. Next it will connect the forms to the back end and roll out the new tags.

**Our organizers** are preparing for an upcoming guest speaker and talked about how to welcome new members. They also connected the Youth Tech Labs Grocr team with the Cambridge Food Bank.

## Coming up

Demo Day is June 4, and planning has started.
