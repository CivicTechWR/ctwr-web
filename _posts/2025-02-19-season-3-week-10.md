## Project updates

**Waterloo Region Votes** started a shared HedgeDoc, a Markdown editor the whole team can write in at once. The team gathered a first batch of voter resources, sketched out a way to collect more of them over time, and added the first event to the website. More events are on the way.

**Connected KW** finished its profile page and is still fixing some bugs. Login works now, and the team has started filling in profiles. The rich text editor is still giving them trouble. Next up are a cleaner page layout, the page that lists every profile, and a form for creating your own profile.

**Connected KW's outreach team** started planning an event for March 5 and is scheduling more meetings. Invitations will go out with a link to the volunteer directory.

**Our Discourse forum** is up, and a welcome post is ready for new members. The next steps are finding more ways to use it, connecting it to the tools we already use, and writing a guide to installing it on your phone.

## Get involved

Waterloo Region Votes needs help with marketing and outreach. The easiest way to help is to share their posts.

Come say hi on [the forum](https://discourse.ctwr.org/), or join us at a Wednesday hacknight.
