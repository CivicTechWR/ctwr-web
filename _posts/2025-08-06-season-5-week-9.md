## Project updates

**One Million Neighbours** worked on finishing the dataset behind its map. The database and the API are done, and the map will show several measures for each neighbourhood. A long conversation about combining those measures into one score to rank neighbourhoods raised hard questions the team hasn't answered yet. Next it will polish the front end and add map layers you can switch on and off to see which neighbourhoods have services and resources and which don't.

**Project Ploughshares** made progress on two scrapers. One collects data from MarketLine, the source Ploughshares uses. The other tracks Google Alerts by keyword and saves the results, which next need to go to the Gemini-based analyzer. After that, the team will package the tools, deploy them for Ploughshares to use, connect them to the database, and get the PDF crawler running.

**PECH** polished the site's main page and is building the data page. Two team members are finalizing its content, and the team knows where the dashboard and the data story will go. The analysis now has three metrics, with more to come, and a plan for showing each one. Next the team will finish the content, catch up with its designer, and finish the analysis so the dashboard can go up.

**Our organizers** are preparing for the showcase and catching up on recaps from the last few weeks.

## Coming up

The project showcase is moving from September 3 to September 10 to line up with Waterloo Tech Week (September 8 to 11). That gives every project one more week.
