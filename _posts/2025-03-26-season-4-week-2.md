This week four ideas were on the table, and everyone voted on which ones to take on this season.

## The pitches

**Midtown Radio** came with a mockup of a mobile app made by their graphic designer. The group talked about making it work well on phones and building a prototype in the open on GitHub. An open source app could also be useful to other community radio stations. This pitch got the most votes, with 11.

**Volunteering with Connected KW** got 9 votes. The team decided to build login next. They also want better skill categories, like the ones on Fiverr or Upwork, plus prompts that help people describe themselves, a request form, an intake process, and messaging.

**PECH**, the Plan to End Chronic Homelessness, got 4 votes. The group agreed the problem needs more research before anyone builds anything. Ideas so far include Power BI, Tableau, a self-hosted Metabase, and helping PECH collect data in new ways. A few volunteers will gather requirements with PECH.

**Food security** got 3 votes. Many of the existing apps have a lot of complaints. The group looked at Tiny Home Takeout and liked the idea of a local version of Vancouver Food Runners.

## The decision

Midtown Radio and Connected KW go ahead this season. PECH and food security move into the pipeline for more research.
