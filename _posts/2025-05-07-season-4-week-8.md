## Project updates

**Midtown Radio** has a fully working app. Next week it should be ready to submit to the app stores.

**One Million Neighbours** is starting from a report and working out how to turn it into something people can use, beginning with which measures of a neighbourhood to build. They also talked about using existing open source tools like OpenStreetMap so the project works well with others, and what a second phase could look like.

**Connected KW** added login and logout buttons and talked about finding sponsors.
