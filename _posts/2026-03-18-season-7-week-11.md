## Project updates

**Accessible Housing** worked on the problems people were having running the project locally and set up a demo environment. The site's text is in progress and will go into the demo next. The team also talked about a new name. It's AHA for now.

**LoMo** reviewed its milestones and open issues and split them among the group. Last week's login problem is fixed, and the team started setting up tokens to help automate its work. Next is handing out tasks, building out the app's screens, and more design work.

**Waterloo Region Votes** went through its to-do list and picked out what has to be ready for the project showcase. The team worked on the structure of the tabbed layout and collected questions for everyone to test during the demo. Next it will assign specific issues to specific people.
