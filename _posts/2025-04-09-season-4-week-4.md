## Project updates

**Midtown Radio** spent the night setting up and walking everyone through the project and the core development concepts behind it. The team is dreaming big. The design assets aren't on GitHub yet. They're meeting on Saturday to keep working on the prototype and hope to have a working demo by next Wednesday.

We learned how **Project Ploughshares** collects its data. The group brainstormed which parts could be automated and how to move off Microsoft Access to a more modern database. We're working with Project Ploughshares to scope what needs to happen before next season.

**Connected KW** cleaned up its code and started moving to the Next.js App Router. Next it will upgrade to the latest Next.js and finish its documentation.
