  - type: cards
    kicker: How decisions get made
    heading: Grassroots, not top-down
    columns: 3
    cards:
      - title: rule_01.txt
        heading: No one to ask for permission
        body: We have no hierarchy. You don't need approval from an organizer to start something.
        note: "**For example:** you want to try a better icebreaker at the start of hacknight. Go for it."
      - title: rule_02.txt
        heading: Seek consensus instead
        body: Talk your idea over with the people it affects. If they're on board, that's your green light.
        note: "**For example:** share your plan in Slack or at the start of hacknight, then listen."
      - title: rule_03.txt
        heading: Lead your own ideas
        body: There's no queue of tasks and no staff to pick up feedback. Ideas move when someone takes them on, and organizers will back you.
        note: "**For example:** you think the website needs a FAQ. Draft one, and we'll help you publish it."

  - type: media_text
    heading: Since 2017
    body: |-
      <p>CivicTechWR started in July 2017, inspired by Civic Tech Toronto, Beta City YEG and the Code for America Brigades. We've helped voters find their ballot, tracked progress on homelessness, and connected people to local events.</p>
      <p>Everything we build is open source and lives on <a href="https://github.com/civictechwr">GitHub</a>. Read our <a href="https://civictechwr.github.io/CTWR-Organization-Documentation/policies/Code_of_Conduct">Code of Conduct</a> and <a href="https://civictechwr.github.io/CTWR-Organization-Documentation/policies/Diversity_and_Inclusion_Policy">Diversity and Inclusion Policy</a>.</p>
