ConnectedKW is a volunteer-run guide to what's happening in Waterloo Region: community events, free activities and local resources, all in one place.

In 2025 a CivicTechWR team built a volunteer directory for ConnectedKW, where local organizations can post requests for help and volunteers can create profiles listing their skills. The team built sign-up and login, profile and request forms, and a tagging system for skills, then rewrote the site's text in plain language before showing it at the season 4 showcase.
