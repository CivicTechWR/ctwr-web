Waterloo Region Votes is a non-partisan guide to municipal and school board elections across the region. Type in your address to see your wards, the races on your ballot and every candidate running in them.

CivicTechWR volunteers first built the site for the 2018 election and have brought it back for every election since. For 2026 the team rebuilt the site around candidate pages, so voters can filter for the races that affect them, and added recordings of all-candidates meetings and candidate questionnaire answers.

Most of the work is people work: emailing candidates, entering their answers, timestamping meeting recordings and putting posters up around the region. You don't need to code to help.
