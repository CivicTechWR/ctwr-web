There's no shortage of music streaming platforms, but finding music made by people in your own region isn't something they do well. Artists were producing music without finding local audiences, and listeners had no central place to find it.

> "There just wasn't a good place to go to hear locally produced music," said David Harmes, who led the project. "Commercial radio rarely plays it, and most people don't know where else to look."

During season 4, a team of volunteers, including students from Conestoga, Waterloo and Guelph and working professionals from across the region, designed and built a mobile app for [MidTown Radio](https://www.midtownradio.ca/). It gives listeners the station's live stream, its podcasts and a directory of local artists.

Twelve weeks later, the app was live on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/midtown-radio/id6746517964) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.civitechwr.midtownradio.app.ctwr_midtown_radio_app). Read the full story on [our blog](/blog/season-4-project-midtown-radio/).
