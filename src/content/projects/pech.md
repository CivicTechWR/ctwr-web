Waterloo Region has a plan to end chronic homelessness by 2030. This project helps the community see how that plan is going: what the data says, which housing programs are running, and the lived experience behind the numbers.

The project was pitched at hacknight in spring 2025 and is built with the people co-creating the plan. The site is open about its data while keeping people anonymous, and pairs dashboards with plain-language stories about what each measure means.

The team includes developers, a designer, data analysts and writers. If you like making sense of messy data, or explaining it clearly, there's a seat for you.
